Stated limitation · 1 attested enclave behind 6 committee seats
A single attested gateway enclave fronts all 6 models, and every registration reports the same measurement 0x3e1ff5ed68…f1029a74. The quotes are real and the verification is real, but what they attest is one machine running one image, not 6 independent ones. A committee is only as independent as the enclaves behind it, so the correlated-failure argument for a multi-model committee does not hold on this deployment: the models differ, the hardware does not. The registry counts distinct signing keys, which is why minDistinctSigners is the figure that actually binds here.

II—Allowlisted images

measurement · admitted in

A verified quote is not enough on its own. The registry admits a key only if the measurement in its quote is an image the operator allowlisted first, in its own transaction — so which software may answer is a decision on the record, made before any key was bound to it.
