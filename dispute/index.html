I—How a challenge settles

01

Challenger wins

The fresh valuation lands further from the contested one than the dispute band allows. The contested value is voided, a halt is recorded, and the bond is returned to the challenger.

02

Challenger wins by silence

The fresh round cannot reach consensus at all. Refusing to price counts as evidence for the challenger, not against them — the same value is voided and the bond comes back.

03

Challenge fails

The fresh valuation lands inside the dispute band. The new value is published, the asset goes back to Live, and the bond is forfeited to the issuer.

II—Claim a bond

credited on settlement · withdrawn separately

Settlement credits the winner rather than paying them. A transfer that fails — a recipient contract that reverts on receipt, or one that runs out of gas — would otherwise be able to hold the whole settlement hostage, so the value is recorded against the address and claimed in its own transaction.

III—Open a challenge

permissionless · anyone may post a bond

Only a Live valuation can be contested; a round that already refused to publish has nothing to argue with. The bond is at risk: if the re-appraisal agrees with the contested value, it goes to the issuer.
