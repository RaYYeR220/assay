I—Settlement record

the same call, on a struck price and on a refused one

Two transactions, both sent, both on chain. The first priced against a valuation the committee agreed on and minted shares. The second is the identical call against an asset the oracle had refused: it was mined, it consumed gas, and it moved nothing, because the chain rejected it. A refusal that costs a real transaction is the only kind worth claiming.
